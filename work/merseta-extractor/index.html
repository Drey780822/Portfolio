merSETA Extractor — AI Navigator: National Skills Registry Workspace

An institutional AI research workspace that automates document ingestion, skills extraction, and occupational intelligence—transforming 40+ page technical sector reports into verified competencies, capability bridges, and grounded citations in seconds.

Role

Lead AI & Frontend Systems Architect

Core Stack

React 19 · TanStack Start (SSR) · Tailwind v4

Live Deployment

merseta-ai.vercel.app ↗

Design Philosophy

LOAM Humanist AI ("The Seed")

1. The Problem It Solves

National qualifications authorities, research institutions, and industrial policy planners in South Africa spend hundreds of hours manually reviewing dense sector skills plans, training curricula, and industrial standards (PDFs, reports).

Manual skills auditing is slow, disconnected from real-time industrial demand (e.g., Industry 4.0, PLC programming, robotics, predictive maintenance), and lacks transparent evidence tracing back to exact source text. Furthermore, traditional black-box LLM summarizers regularly hallucinate non-existent competencies without audit-grade proof.

2. The 4-Stage Progressive Document Pipeline

merSETA Extractor replaces manual auditing with a deterministic 4-stage pipeline that delivers transparent evidence grounding:

[ PIPELINE // 4-STAGE-EXTRACTION ] Progressive Ingestion to Grounded Citations 
[Document Dropzone] (PDF, DOCX, CSV, TXT up to 20MB)
       │
       ▼
(Stage 1: Reading) ───────► Normalizes raw layout & text streams
       │
       ▼
(Stage 2: Identifying) ───► Isolates emerging competencies & OFO occupations
       │
       ▼
(Stage 3: Structuring) ───► Synthesizes competency matrices & confidence scores (e.g. 94%)
       │
       ▼
(Stage 4: Ready) ─────────► Activates Split-View Reader, Capability Bridges & Citations
  • Document Ingestion: Drag-and-drop intake supporting PDF, DOCX, CSV, and TXT files up to 20MB.
  • Competency Extraction & Evidence Grounding: Automatically detects high-demand engineering skills, assigns verification confidence scores (e.g. 94% verified), and provides direct citations (exact section, page number, and source quote).
  • Capability Bridge Mapping: Connects current technician capabilities to high-demand Industry 4.0 roles (Systems Integration, PLC Programming, SCADA, Robotics, Predictive Maintenance).
  • Grounded Conversational AI: An unboxed research chat interface where users query ingested documents with citation badges, evidence source trays, and interactive verification.
  • Interactive Document Reader: A synchronized split-view document reader with zoom controls and section outline navigation.

3. LOAM Design System & Humanist AI Philosophy

Built upon the LOAM design language ("Earth, not tech. A pebble, not a person"), inspired by Mustafa Suleyman's philosophy of calm, honest AI that serves humans without pretending to possess consciousness:

  • Zero Performed Sentience: No fake emojis, animated humanoid faces, or artificial pleasantries. Responses are unboxed, grounded, and evidence-first.
  • "The Seed" Presence Component: A smooth, superellipse pebble utilizing subtle continuous animation curves to communicate 5 genuine system states:
    • idle: 5-second slow breathing curve.
    • listening: edge ripples outward as user engages input.
    • thinking: single Tide ring drifts organically around the perimeter.
    • responding: soft pulse on leading edge of generation.
    • calm error: gentle desaturation without intrusive alarm animations.
  • Material Color Ramp: Grounded in tactile elements—Daylight on Sand (#F4EFE6), Night Soil (#1A1611), Clay (#B5532F, human action), Moss (#5B7A3A, verified skills), Tide (#2F6F7E, system voice/citations), Ochre (#C8932B, inferred bridges), and Madder (#A8362F, error states).
  • Humanist Typography: Fraunces (warm serif headlines), Source Sans 3 (UI body text), and JetBrains Mono (tabular telemetry and exact citations).

5. Real-World Results & Impact

In production, merSETA Extractor transforms the speed and quality of national workforce analysis:

  • Instant Document Intelligence: Ingests and structures 40+ page dense industrial curriculum and sector plans in seconds rather than days of manual reading.
  • 100% Traceable Evidence: Eliminates hallucinations by providing clickable citations that highlight the exact paragraph and page number in the source text.
