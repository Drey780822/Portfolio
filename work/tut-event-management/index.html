TUT Event Management System

A centralized campus event discovery platform featuring tamper-evident QR-code e-ticketing and automated confirmation notifications via WhatsApp and email.

1. Problem Statement & Campus Motivation

At Tshwane University of Technology (eMalahleni campus), student event announcements were historically dispersed across paper noticeboard posters, informal social media pages, and crowded WhatsApp group chats.

This fragmentation caused numerous recurring problems:

  • Students frequently missed academic workshops, career fairs, and departmental hackathons.
  • Event organizers had no mechanism to accurately forecast attendance or enforce venue safety capacities.
  • Paper admission tickets were easily duplicated, leading to gate security disputes and unauthorized venue access.

2. The TriTech Solutions Architecture

As team leader of TriTech Solutions, I identified this structural bottleneck and led a 3-developer team to engineer a unified web portal:

  • Event Discovery & Filtering: Students can explore upcoming verified university events, filter by academic faculty or social category, and view real-time seat availability.
  • Authenticated Student Registration: Students reserve tickets using verified university emails, establishing attendance accountability.
  • Cryptographic QR E-Ticketing: Each ticket reservation generates a uniquely signed QR code containing a cryptographically verifiable token. Gate marshals scan tickets with smartphone cameras to invalidate single-use passes instantly.
  • Omnichannel Alerts: Automated booking confirmations and calendar invites are dispatched via SMTP email and WhatsApp webhook integrations.

4. My Role & Leadership Contribution

I acted in a dual capacity as Team Leader and Primary Backend Engineer:

  • Backend Development: Designed the complete relational schema in PostgreSQL, written with SQLAlchemy models enforcing capacity thresholds through database row-level locks to prevent overbooking race conditions.
  • QR Token Engine: Engineered the cryptographic ticketing service generating HMAC-signed tokens embedded into SVG and PNG QR representations.
  • Team Leadership: Facilitated sprint planning, established git feature-branching conventions, and conducted technical reviews on our front-end JavaScript implementations.

5. Verified Results & Deployment Status

The application is deployed live in production on Vercel at mytutevents.vercel.app. It provides a real-world working template for modern campus logistics, eliminating physical printing costs while ensuring gate ticket validation integrity.
