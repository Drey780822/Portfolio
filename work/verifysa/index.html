VerifySA: AI-Assisted Document Verification

A document verification and trust-scoring prototype combining multimodal LLM extraction with deterministic business-rule validation, built in an intensive 5-hour hackathon sprint.

1. Project Overview & Hackathon Sprint

At the Soweto AI App Development Challenge (June 2026), teams were challenged to conceptualize, architect, code, and deploy an impactful AI-enabled solution within a single five-hour sprint.

As Team Lead of a 10-person developer team, I coordinated task allocation, system architecture, repository branching, and deployment orchestration to build VerifySA: a document authentication prototype designed for small businesses and institutions plagued by forged certificates, falsified utility bills, and altered invoices.

2. The Core Concept: Multimodal AI + Deterministic Rules

A fundamental engineering principle guided our design: Large Language Models are powerful at extracting unstructured data, but they must never be the sole authority for fraud detection.

VerifySA employs a two-tier verification pipeline:

  • Tier 1 — Multimodal Semantic Extraction (OpenAI GPT-4o): Ingests high-resolution document scans, extracts structural metadata (names, national ID numbers, tax references, issue dates, institution letterheads), and detects visual tampering anomalies.
  • Tier 2 — Deterministic Rule Validation Engine: Pipes the extracted JSON structure through hard-coded deterministic rules (Luhn algorithm validation on South African ID numbers, date sequencing sanity checks, tax number length checks, and known institution registry lookups).

4. Team Leadership & Contribution Scope

Leading 10 developers across 5 hours required rigorous coordination:

  • Task Allocation: Divided the team into three specialized sub-groups: Frontend (Upload UI & Dashboard, 4 devs), Backend & Schema (Supabase & Rule Engine, 3 devs), and AI Integration & PDF Generation (3 devs).
  • Technical Architecture: Established the Next.js 15 repository scaffold, defined TypeScript interfaces for document schema exchanges, and orchestrated GitHub pull requests to prevent merge conflicts under deadline pressure.
  • Deployment Execution: Configured edge deployments on Vercel and connected live environment secrets, ensuring the working application was fully accessible for the judging panel.

5. Critical Disclosure & Engineering Honesty

Caveat on AI in Fraud Prevention: VerifySA is an innovative hackathon proof-of-concept. AI extraction flags potential irregularities, but AI analysis alone does not legally prove a document is authentic. Real-world commercial deployment requires integration with official national population registers (e.g. Home Affairs API) and accredited digital signature validators. We make no unverified claims of commercial fraud accuracy rates without formal benchmark validation.
